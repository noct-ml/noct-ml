*Exports ChatGPT conversations with structured formatting*

## Panasonic RR-DR60 Emulator
*DSP-based simulation of a vintage voice recorder*

Replicates the low-fidelity audio characteristics of the Panasonic RR-DR60, including compression artifacts, voice activation glitches, and noise—ideal for EVP research, retro audio effects, or DSP experimentation.

> *Reviving 1990s hardware quirks for modern analysis.*

[→ Panasonic RR-DR60 Emulator](https://github.com/noct-ml/panasonic-rr-dr60-emulator)
